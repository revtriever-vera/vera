Wealth

Brazil, US and crypto in a single currency, compared with the portfolio you consider ideal.

Wealth management

Register each asset with its quantity. Vera computes the value, adds it up in reais or dollars and shows where the portfolio is off target.

  • Two-layer target portfolio, with a tolerance band.
  • Where to invest: your contribution split across the classes below target, selling nothing.
  • Fixed income with real yield, maturities and FGC coverage per banking group.

Spending

Card and account through Open Finance, categorized, so you can see where to cut.

Spending management

Set how much of your income you want to save. Vera computes the shortfall and spreads the cut across the flexible categories that went furthest over your average.

  • Monthly summary and transactions with category rules.
  • Where to cut: a suggested limit per category.
  • Connection through Meu Pluggy, with your own free account.

Ask Vera, import and portfolio tracking

Three ways Vera helps beyond the screens. It explains and shows your numbers; the decision is always yours.

Ask Vera

  • A question panel, open from any screen.
  • Free: help on how to use Vera.
  • Premium: answers about your spending and portfolio and about the market (CDI, IPCA, Selic, Ibovespa, assets and funds), with your permission to send data.
  • Actions such as creating a goal or categorizing a transaction only happen after you confirm.

Investment import

  • B3 report (.xlsx or CSV) and crypto CSV from Binance, Mercado Bitcoin and Coinbase, on Free.
  • Premium: brokerage note as PDF, broker screenshot and monthly Avenue statement as PDF, read by a language model.
  • You check the lines and the effect on the portfolio before saving.

Portfolio tracking

  • Portfolio agenda and the monthly X-ray, with the month's numbers, on Free.
  • Premium: "What changed", with summaries of the official documents (CVM) of the assets you hold, and the written paragraph of the X-ray.

Vera does not recommend buying or selling any asset. It explains, compares and shows the portfolio against the target you set.
